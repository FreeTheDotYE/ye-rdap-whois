# Data rights and responsible use

This repository contains normalized public technical facts and a complete archive of selected public protocol responses and CT artifacts. The maintainers do not claim exclusive ownership of underlying facts.

The code license does not grant rights in third-party databases, marks, or other material that may be implicated by a user’s jurisdiction or use case. Users are responsible for evaluating applicable database rights, contractual restrictions, attribution expectations, and laws. This notice is not legal advice.

The normalized data layer excludes bulk personal-contact fields. The raw observation layer preserves complete public RDAP responses. Do not use the dataset to harass people, infer personal identity, attribute wrongdoing, or bypass access controls. Technical overlap, status, absence, or a query mismatch does not establish ownership, affiliation, intent, or illegality.

Corrections supported by public, non-personal technical evidence are welcome. Requests involving personal data or security-sensitive material should use the private process described in SECURITY.md.
