# Data rights and responsible use

This repository contains a privacy-minimized normalization of public technical facts and public protocol outcomes. The maintainers do not claim exclusive ownership of underlying facts.

The code license does not grant rights in third-party databases, marks, or other material that may be implicated by a user’s jurisdiction or use case. Users are responsible for evaluating applicable database rights, contractual restrictions, attribution expectations, and laws. This notice is not legal advice.

The release excludes bulk personal-contact fields and raw responses. Do not use the dataset to harass people, infer personal identity, attribute wrongdoing, or bypass access controls. Technical overlap, status, absence, or a query mismatch does not establish ownership, affiliation, intent, or illegality.

Corrections supported by public, non-personal technical evidence are welcome. Requests involving personal data or security-sensitive material should use the private process described in SECURITY.md.
